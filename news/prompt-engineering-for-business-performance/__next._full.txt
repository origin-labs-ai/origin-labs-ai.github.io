Prompt chaining</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Sometimes Claude performs better on complex tasks if you break the task down into multiple prompts corresponding to each step. This is known as ‘prompt chaining’.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Prompt chaining allows you to iterate on a prompt over multiple steps. Each new prompt can include the previous prompt-response pairs to build on the context. This technique enables you to guide Claude through a process by repeatedly prompting, responding, and expanding the prompt with each interaction.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">For example, if you want Claude to help with explaining tax situations, you could first prompt it to create a list of the tax codes that are related to the specific question, then prompt Claude to identify the relevant sections in each document, and finally, to respond to a user question based on the information Claude’s gathered.</p><h2 class="tOQyXa_headline-4 PA4F1q_reading-column post-heading" data-component="Headline" id="case-study-prompting-for-enhanced-accuracy-and-speed">Case study: Prompting for enhanced accuracy and speed</h2><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">A Fortune 500 company wanted to build an AI-powered chat assistant that could respond to customers’ questions about some unique and complex issues.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">The company tried some other solutions in the market, but were unhappy with their wordiness, stilted tone, and overall lack of cohesion. They also wanted to see if they could achieve lower latency — i.e., provide faster responses — without affecting accuracy.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">They turned to <a href="/news/releasing-claude-instant-1-2">Claude Instant</a> to power a friendly, concise chatbot that could answer customers’ questions quickly and accurately.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">The company’s first efforts to deploy Claude fell short of their goal for accuracy. To remedy this, we sent in an Anthropic prompt engineer to partner with the company’s subject matter experts on improving Claude’s responses.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Our prompt engineers applied several techniques to improve Claude’s outputs, including:</p><ul class="PA4F1q_reading-column text-body-1-serif post-text"><li>Telling Claude to use a scratchpad to show its work (customers don’t see the scratchpad, but it helps improve Claude’s accuracy)</li><li>Providing few-shot examples of good answers, with a focus on training Claude to use the company’s desired format and style</li><li>Directing Claude to use the data points and workflow recommended by subject matter experts (SMEs) — in this case, a set of factors that affect a customer’s legal situation</li></ul><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Below is an example prompt to help bring this guidance to life. You help users understand the details of the tax code.</em></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><em>Here is the relevant section of the tax code.<br>&lt;tax_code&gt;<br>{{TAX_CODE}}<br>&lt;/tax_code&gt;</em></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><em>Here are some examples of questions and answers about this section of the tax code:</em></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><em>&lt;examples&gt;<br>&lt;example&gt;<br>&lt;question&gt;<br>{{EXAMPLE QUESTION 1}}<br>&lt;/question&gt;<br>&lt;answer&gt;<br>{{EXAMPLE ANSWER 1}}<br>&lt;/answer&gt;<br>&lt;/example&gt;<br>…<br>&lt;/examples&gt;</em></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><em>Now here is the user's question about the tax code that I’d like you to answer:<br>&lt;question&gt;{{QUESTION}}&lt;/question&gt;</em></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><em>First, pull relevant quotes from the tax code in &lt;relevant_quotes&gt; tags. Then write a concise, factual response to the user’s question in &lt;answer&gt; tags. Your answer should be fully grounded in the relevant quotes from the tax code that you extracted.</em></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><em>Assistant: &lt;relevant_quotes&gt;"</em></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">By combining our prompt engineers’ knowledge of prompting best practices with the customer’s subject matter expertise, we <strong>improved Claude’s accuracy by 20%</strong> - and helped get their product to market faster and at a lower cost.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="getting-started-with-prompt-engineering">Getting started with prompt engineering</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">There’s no single best technique for prompt engineering.
