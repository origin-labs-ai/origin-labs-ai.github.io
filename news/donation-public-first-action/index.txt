data-theme="ivory"><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">We're contributing an additional $20 million to <a id="base-ui-_R_v1drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://publicfirstaction.us/">Public First Action</a>, bringing our total support to $40 million. Public First Action is a nonpartisan organization that educates the public about AI and works with Republicans, Democrats, and Independents who are serious about putting sensible AI safeguards in place. Both of our donations were made exclusively to support Public First Action’s public education and policy mission, and cannot be used to influence the election of any candidate for federal, state, or local office.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Our first donation in February 2026 was made to help promote policies that will maintain meaningful safeguards, sustain America’s AI leadership, and demand transparency from the companies building the most powerful AI models. In the months since, the case for these policies has only <a id="base-ui-_R_v2drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://darioamodei.com/post/policy-on-the-ai-exponential">gotten stronger</a>.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="why-now">Why now?</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">AI models continue to improve at a rapid pace. Earlier this year, Claude Mythos Preview <a id="base-ui-_R_1t4drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://red.anthropic.com/2026/mythos-preview/">discovered</a> thousands of high-severity software vulnerabilities, including some in every major operating system and browser. We chose to release it to a limited set of cyber defenders through <a href="/glasswing">Project Glasswing</a> to enable trusted actors to find and fix those weaknesses before anyone could exploit them. In the wrong hands, models like this could threaten the critical systems the country relies on, from hospitals to the energy grid.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">More capable models will bring benefits—like compressing drug discovery, developing treatments for diseases we’ve never been able to treat, accelerating science to deliver a century of progress in a decade, extending human lifespans, and driving the kind of economic growth that lets everyone share in the prosperity—but we need to make sure we are protected against the risks first in order to realize those benefits.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Governments need time to build the capacity to capture benefits while containing risks, which is why they need to start now.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="policy-that-meets-the-moment">Policy that meets the moment</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">We’ve long argued that frontier AI companies should be transparent about what their models can do and how they’re managing the risks. We’ve <a href="/news/the-need-for-transparency-in-frontier-ai">supported</a> newly passed laws in several states that require greater transparency for AI developers. But given how fast the capabilities of the most powerful models are advancing, transparency alone is insufficient.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">We need policymakers and candidates to put forward measures that mitigate risks. This is the core of what we’ve laid out in <a href="/policy-on-the-ai-exponential/aaif">Anthropic's Advanced AI Framework</a>, which is the strongest policy proposal from any frontier lab or policymaker to date. Governments should be able to verify companies’ safety claims, enforce safe practices through civil penalties, and ultimately have a way to slow or block the deployment of AI models that pose a serious risk of catastrophic harm. Frontier AI developers should have to test models that pose catastrophic risk, be transparent to the public about their findings, submit them to independent evaluation, and maintain a robust security program.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">As the pace of AI advances accelerates, the national security stakes of AI are growing, as we outlined in <a href="/research/2028-ai-leadership">2028: Two Scenarios for Global AI Leadership</a>. America and our democratic allies have the advantage today, but that lead is tenuous. To ensure continued American AI leadership, we support policy efforts to tighten export controls on advanced chips and semiconductor manufacturing equipment and to curb illicit model access and distillation attacks, so that democracies’ best technologies are not used to advance authoritarian AI.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="what-comes-next">What comes next?</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">These <a href="/policy-on-the-ai-exponential/aaif">policy frameworks</a> are just a starting point. Our donation to Public First Action is one way in which we’re trying to raise the salience of this urgent policy debate.</p></div></div></article></div><!--$--><!--/$-->4:["$","div",null,{"className":"cloned-page","data-slug":"news/donation-public-first-action","children":[null,["$","a",null,{"href":"#main-content","className":"aB5J5q_root","data-skip-link":"true","children":"Skip to main content"}],["$","$Le",null,{"hideOnScroll":false,"overlay":false}],["$","main",null,{"id":"main-content","children":["$","$Lf",null,{"desktop":"$10","mobile":"$undefined"}]}],"$L11","$L12"]}]
