Today’s agreement expands our collaboration in three ways.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><strong>Infrastructure at scale.</strong> We are committing more than $100 billion over the next ten years to AWS technologies, securing up to 5GW of new capacity to train and run Claude. We continue to choose AWS as our primary training and cloud provider for mission-critical workloads.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">“Our custom AI silicon offers high performance at significantly lower cost for customers, which is why it’s in such hot demand,” said Andy Jassy, CEO of Amazon. “Anthropic's commitment to run its large language models on AWS Trainium for the next decade reflects the progress we've made together on custom silicon, as we continue delivering the technology and infrastructure our customers need to build with generative AI.”</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><strong>Claude Platform on AWS.</strong> The full Claude Platform will be available directly within AWS. Same account, same controls, same billing, with more Claude Platform features and no additional credentials or contracts necessary. Claude remains the only frontier AI model available to customers on all three of the world's largest cloud platforms: AWS (Bedrock), Google Cloud (Vertex AI), and Microsoft Azure (Foundry). Claude Platform on AWS is coming soon.
