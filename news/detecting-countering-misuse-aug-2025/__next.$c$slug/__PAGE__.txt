Rather than encrypt the stolen information with traditional ransomware, the actor threatened to expose the data publicly in order to attempt to extort victims into paying ransoms that sometimes exceeded $500,000.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">The actor used AI to what we believe is an unprecedented degree.
