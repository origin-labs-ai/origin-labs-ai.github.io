id="why-were-building-for-teachers">Why we’re building for teachers</h2><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Decades of research show that practices like differentiation, mastery-based learning, and small group instruction reliably improve student achievement, but teachers are often short on time and resources to implement them. Budgets are stretched, classes may be too large to meet every student's individual needs, and planning often spills into evenings. This strain is heaviest in under-resourced schools. Claude for Teachers is designed to close the gap between educational best practices and what a teacher's week allows.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><a id="base-ui-_R_1t4drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://scale.stanford.edu/research-in-action/understanding-evidence-base-ai-k12-education" rel="noopener noreferrer">Early evidence</a> suggests that while the impact of AI tools for students is mixed and depends on the implementation, AI tools for teachers can strengthen instructional practice and improve student outcomes. This is the aim of Claude for Teachers: support the craft behind great teaching and protect what teachers value most—time with their students.</p><h2 class="tOQyXa_headline-4 PA4F1q_reading-column post-heading" data-component="Headline" id="connected-to-evidence-based-curricula-and-the-k-12-ecosystem">Connected to evidence-based curricula and the K-12 ecosystem</h2><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Claude for Teachers connects to <a id="base-ui-_R_3q6drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://learningcommons.org" rel="noopener noreferrer">Learning Commons</a>, giving Claude access to academic standards across all 50 states—and beneath each standard, the smaller learning competencies it's built from and the order students typically learn them. So when Claude drafts a lesson plan, it’s scaffolded and aligned to teaching standards. Claude for Teachers also brings in trusted curricular resources like <a id="base-ui-_R_3s6drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://openscied.org/" rel="noopener noreferrer">OpenSciEd</a> and IM v.360 from <a id="base-ui-_R_3u6drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://illustrativemathematics.org/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Illustrative Mathematics</a>.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">As of today, educators can now connect Claude across an entire ecosystem of K-12 tools.</p><ul class="PA4F1q_reading-column text-body-1-serif post-text"><li><a id="base-ui-_R_uh8drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://www.assistments.org/" rel="noopener feedback grounded in real classroom talk.</li></ul><h2 class="tOQyXa_headline-4 PA4F1q_reading-column post-heading" data-component="Headline" id="how-it-works">How it works</h2><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Once verified, K-12 educators in the US get access to Claude for Teachers with the Learning Commons connector and a set of tailored teaching skills grounded in learning science. The skills, co-developed with Learning Commons, were designed around the tasks teachers told us mattered most. These were evaluated to ensure rigor, pedagogical alignment, and classroom usability, and refined through early feedback from classroom teachers, including those in schools like Prospect Schools in Brooklyn.</p><ul class="PA4F1q_reading-column text-body-1-serif post-text"><li><strong>Plan a lesson from high-quality instructional materials.</strong> Ask for a lesson and Claude draws on widely used curricula mapped to your state's standards, as well as fine-grained learning components and progressions beneath them, then drafts a plan and student-facing materials you can revise and take into class.</li><li><strong>Differentiate for every learner in your room.</strong> Ask Claude to adapt materials for students at different readiness levels and it builds a differentiation plan for you, plus personalized student-facing materials for each proficiency level. The scaffolds make materials accessible to students, while students who are ready can be further challenged.</li></ul><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Claude for Teachers includes Claude Code and Cowork, which means Claude can carry work forward on its own. Some examples:</p><ul class="PA4F1q_reading-column text-body-1-serif post-text"><li><strong>Analyze class materials to plan instruction.</strong> Hand Claude a folder of your instructional materials—as well as your de-identified diagnostics, exit tickets, and notes—and it builds a clear picture of how your classroom is performing, allowing teachers to better tailor instruction. You control what data is shared. Nothing you share is used in model training.</li><li><strong>Schedule repeated tasks.</strong> Hand off a task once—like reviewing each day's de-identified exit tickets to see what students mastered and adapting tomorrow's plan to match—and it runs every school day at 4pm. Claude works while you drive home.</li></ul><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><a id="base-ui-_R_1tedrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://claude.com/resources/tutorials/claude-for-teachers-in-action" rel="noopener noreferrer">Learn more from two teachers</a>, Zac and Karina, explaining how they use Claude for Teachers in their own classrooms.</p><h2 class="tOQyXa_headline-4 PA4F1q_reading-column post-heading" data-component="Headline" id="for-educators-built-to-protect-student-data">For educators, built to protect student data</h2><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Claude for Teachers is for educators only, consistent with Claude's 18-and-over policy. It comes with its own <a id="base-ui-_R_1ugdrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15926041" rel="noopener noreferrer">teacher terms</a>, built for K-12 privacy: </p><ul class="PA4F1q_reading-column text-body-1-serif post-text"><li><strong>We never train our models on your Claude for Teacher conversations.</strong> Training is off for verified teacher accounts.</li><li><strong>Student information is protected by our K-12 Data Processing Addendum</strong>, including FERPA-aligned data protections.</li></ul><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">We’re also working with the<strong> <a id="base-ui-_R_7qidrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://www.aft.org/" rel="noopener noreferrer">American Federation of Teachers</a></strong> to align our terms and privacy practices with gold standards they’re developing:</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">“We've been working with Anthropic on a Gold Standard that sets out industry best practices for safety and privacy in K-12 education,” said Randi Weingarten, President of the American Federation of Teachers. “It’s important that Anthropic is committing to these principles in their new Claude for Teachers — a tool designed by and for educators to assist them instructionally and hopefully give them more time for the human relationships at the heart of learning.”<br><br>Whether and how educational data may be used in Claude for Teachers is determined by your district and state policies.
