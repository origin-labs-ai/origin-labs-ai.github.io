With the expansion of our partnership, it’s embedding Claude across its own business and engineering platforms, scaling a Claude-certified workforce as part of its new Frontier Certified workforce model, and becoming a Global Premier Partner in the <a id="base-ui-_R_v2drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://claude.com/partners">Claude Partner Network</a>.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Successfully integrating AI into a large enterprise requires knowledge of the company's industry, the systems it already runs on, and the rules it operates under. We bring the industry context, the engineering scale and the trust frameworks that use Claude to deliver production outcomes inside the most demanding enterprise environments. This partnership with Anthropic is about doing that for clients who need AI they can rely on, not just experiment with."</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">"Deepening our partnership with Cognizant will help more companies harness AI's growing capability and deploy it in real, practical ways for their businesses," said Daniela Amodei, Co-Founder and President of Anthropic.
