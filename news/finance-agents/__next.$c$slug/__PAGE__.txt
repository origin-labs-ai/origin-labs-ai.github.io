Firms can adapt any of them to their own modeling conventions, risk policies, and approval flows.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Enable these new agent templates either as plugins within Claude Cowork or Claude Code, or as cookbooks for Claude Managed Agents. record;</li><li><strong>Month-end closer</strong> runs the close checklist, prepares journal entries, and produces close reports;</li><li><strong>Statement auditor</strong> reviews financial statements for consistency, completeness, and audit-readiness;</li><li><strong>KYC screener</strong> assembles entity files, reviews source documents, and packages escalations for compliance
