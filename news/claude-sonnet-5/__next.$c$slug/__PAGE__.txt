data-article-body="true" data-theme="ivory"><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Claude Sonnet 5 is built to be the most agentic Sonnet model yet. It can make plans, use tools like browsers and terminals, and run autonomously at a level that, just a few months ago, required larger and more expensive models.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">For many developers, the agentic AI era began with Sonnet-class models: Claude Sonnet 3.5, 3.6, and 3.7 were the first models that showed impressive skills in coding and tool use. More recently, though, the clearest gains in agentic capabilities have been in our Opus-class models.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Sonnet 5 narrows the gap: its performance is close to that of Opus 4.8, but at lower prices. It’s a substantial improvement over its predecessor, Sonnet 4.6, on important aspects of agentic performance like reasoning, tool use, coding, and knowledge work:</p><div class="PA4F1q_media-column" data-type="image" data-width="full-width"><figure class="Duq99q_e-imageWithCaption"><img alt="Claude Sonnet 5 benchmark table" loading="eager" width="2600" height="1234" decoding="async" data-nimg="1" style="color:transparent" src="https://origin-ten-jet.vercel.app/sites/anthropic/pages-assets/64572d1f1f3e72c0ed99.webp"><figcaption class="RMuY0W_root -LJkNq_container -LJkNq_container--default" data-component="RichTextWrapper"><div class="tOQyXa_caption-1 -LJkNq_text" data-component="Caption"><em>Scores for Sonnet 5 on a variety of evaluations compared to those of Sonnet 4.6 and Opus 4.8 (a more generally capable model, for reference). The <a href="/claude-sonnet-5-system-card">Claude Sonnet 5 System Card</a> reports a broader set of evaluations in detail.</em></div></figcaption></figure></div><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Our safety assessments found that Sonnet 5 shows an overall lower rate of undesirable behaviors than Sonnet 4.6, and is generally safer to use in agentic contexts. Evaluations also show that it has a much lower ability to perform cybersecurity tasks than our current Opus models.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">From today, Claude Sonnet 5 is available across all plans: it is the default model for Free and Pro plans, and is available to Max, Team, and Enterprise users. It is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Developers can use <code class="nsPAba_code">claude-sonnet-5</code> via the <a id="base-ui-_R_3s6drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/overview">Claude API</a>.</p><h2 class="tOQyXa_headline-4 PA4F1q_reading-column post-heading" data-component="Headline" id="working-with-claude-sonnet-5">Working with Claude Sonnet 5</h2><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">The charts below compare the performance of Sonnet 5 with Sonnet 4.6 and Opus 4.8 at different <a id="base-ui-_R_3q8drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/effort">effort</a> levels on the agentic search evaluation <a id="base-ui-_R_3s8drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12516">BrowseComp</a> and the computer use evaluation <a id="base-ui-_R_3u8drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://xlang.ai/blog/osworld-verified">OSWorld-Verified</a>.
