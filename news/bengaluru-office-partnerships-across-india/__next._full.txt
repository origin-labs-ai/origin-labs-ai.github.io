Six months ago, we launched a company-wide effort to narrow this gap by curating higher-quality, more representative training data in 10 of the most widely spoken languages throughout India: Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Punjabi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, and Urdu. This resulted in improvements to our models, and we continue to work on enhancing their fluency.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Now, Anthropic is working with <a id="base-ui-_R_7i6drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://www.karya.in/">Karya</a> and the <a id="base-ui-_R_7k6drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://www.cip.org/">Collective Intelligence Project</a> to build <a id="base-ui-_R_7m6drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://weval.org/pilot/india-multilingual">evaluations</a> testing performance on locally relevant tasks across domains like agriculture and law, in partnership with domain experts from leading Indian nonprofits, including <a id="base-ui-_R_7o6drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://digitalgreen.org/">Digital Green</a> and <a id="base-ui-_R_7q6drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://www.adalat.ai/">Adalat AI</a>. At <a id="base-ui-_R_7ibdrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://razorpay.com/">Razorpay</a>, AI is integrated into risk systems, decision-making processes, and operations across the company. <a id="base-ui-_R_7kbdrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://claude.com/customers/rocket"><u class="PA4F1q_underline">Rocket </u></a>uses Claude to let non-technical teams across enterprises build production-ready apps and websites in minutes and hours rather than weeks. At<a id="base-ui-_R_7mbdrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://www.enterpret.com/"><u class="PA4F1q_underline"> Enterpret</u></a>, Claude powers its AI assistant, the engineering team builds with Claude Code daily, and the startup has shipped an MCP integration that brings customer insights directly into Claude. And <a id="base-ui-_R_7obdrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://app.emergent.sh/landing/"><u class="PA4F1q_underline">Emergent</u></a>, an AI-powered platform that lets anyone build software by describing what they want in plain language, reached $25 million in annual recurring revenue and two million users in under five months, built entirely with Claude.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="reaching-students-in-low-income-communities">Reaching students in low-income communities</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Educational and instructional tasks make up 12% of Claude.ai use in India. <a id="base-ui-_R_1uddrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://claude.com/customers/pratham-international" rel="noopener noreferrer">Pratham</a>, one of India’s largest education nonprofits, chose Anthropic as its first strategic AI lab partner because of our shared focus on safety and educational rigor. Their Anytime Testing Machine, powered by Claude, is currently being piloted with 1,500 students across 20 schools, with plans to expand to 100 schools by the end of 2026. Adapted earlier this year for over 5,000 learners in Pratham’s Second Chance program, which supports women who have dropped out of formal schooling, the Anytime Testing Machine aims to create flexible, credible pathways for learning and certification by helping students practice for exams.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Anthropic is collaborating with <a id="base-ui-_R_1uedrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://www.centralsquarefoundation.org/">Central Square Foundation</a> to use EdTech and AI more effectively to educate children from underserved communities. Anthropic is partnering with the <a id="base-ui-_R_7igdrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://ekstep.org/">EkStep Foundation</a> to explore how AI can build on these efforts and deliver population-scale impact in the domains that matter most to India.
