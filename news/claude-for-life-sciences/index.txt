data-article-body="true" data-theme="ivory"><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Increasing the rate of scientific progress is a core part of Anthropic’s public benefit mission.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">We are focused on building the tools to allow researchers to make new discoveries – and eventually, to allow AI models to make these discoveries autonomously.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Until recently, scientists typically used Claude for individual tasks, like writing code for statistical analysis or summarizing papers. Pharmaceutical companies and others in industry also use it for tasks across the rest of their business, like sales, to fund new research. Now, our goal is to make Claude capable of supporting the entire process, from early discovery through to translation and commercialization.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">To do this, we’re rolling out <a id="base-ui-_R_3s4drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://www.claude.com/solutions/life-sciences" rel="noopener noreferrer">several improvements</a> that aim to make Claude a better partner for those who work in the life sciences, including researchers, clinical coordinators, and regulatory affairs managers. </p><h2 class="tOQyXa_headline-4 PA4F1q_reading-column post-heading" data-component="Headline" id="making-claude-a-better-research-partner">Making Claude a better research partner</h2><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">First, we’ve improved Claude’s underlying performance. Our most capable model, Claude Sonnet 4.5, is significantly better than previous models at a range of life sciences tasks. For example, on Protocol QA, a benchmark that tests the model’s understanding and facility with laboratory protocols, Sonnet 4.5 scores 0.83, against a human baseline of 0.79, and Sonnet 4’s performance of 0.74.<sup class="caption PA4F1q_sup PA4F1q_footnote-marker"><a href="#footnote-1" id="footnote-ref-1" class="PA4F1q_footnote-ref" role="doc-noteref" aria-label="Footnote 1">1</a></sup> Sonnet 4.5 shows a similar improvement on its predecessor on BixBench, an evaluation that measures its performance on bioinformatics tasks.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">To make Claude more useful for scientific work, we’re now adding several <a id="base-ui-_R_3s7drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://claude.com/partners/mcp" rel="noopener noreferrer">new connectors</a> to scientific platforms, the ability to use Agent Skills, and life sciences-specific support in the form of a prompt library and dedicated support.</p><h2 class="tOQyXa_headline-4 PA4F1q_reading-column post-heading" data-component="Headline" id="connecting-claude-to-scientific-tools">Connecting Claude to scientific tools</h2><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><a id="base-ui-_R_3q9drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://claude.ai/settings/connectors" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Connectors</strong></a> allow Claude to access other platforms and tools directly.
