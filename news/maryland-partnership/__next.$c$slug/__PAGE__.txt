Under the new partnership, the state will deploy Claude across multiple state agencies to address several priorities:</p><ul class="PA4F1q_reading-column text-body-1-serif post-text"><li><strong>Connecting families with benefits</strong>: A Claude-powered virtual assistant will help residents apply for essential benefits, including SNAP, Medicaid, temporary cash assistance, and WIC. Under our new partnership, caseworkers will use Claude to more quickly and accurately verify these documents, validate people’s eligibility, and provide policy guidance for complex cases.</li><li><strong>Upskilling workers and supporting essential community services</strong>: Maryland will explore an AI upskilling pilot for early-career professionals in Maryland’s <a id="base-ui-_R_3rqdrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://business.maryland.gov/roadmap/">“lighthouse” industries</a> to support the state’s economic priorities. Anthropic will also work with the Governor's Innovation Team on a new tool that uses Claude to identify unmet market<em> </em>needs in local communities, like fresh food and childcare, and equips local leaders and organizations with the data, analysis and resources they need to help address these needs.</li></ul><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">The partnership builds on Maryland’s existing use of Claude to improve its services. Our teams, alongside the Rockefeller Foundation and Percepta, have since worked to develop programs that meet Maryland’s needs and can serve as a model for other states across the country.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Anthropic’s commitment to responsible AI deployment, including rigorous safety testing, makes Claude uniquely suited for sensitive government applications like these.
