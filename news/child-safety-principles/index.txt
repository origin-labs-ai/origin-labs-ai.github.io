class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Alongside other leading AI companies, we are committed to implementing robust child safety measures in the development, deployment, and maintenance of generative AI technologies. This new initiative, led by <a id="base-ui-_R_1t1drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://thorn.org">Thorn</a>, a nonprofit dedicated to defending children from sexual abuse, and <a id="base-ui-_R_1u1drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://alltechishuman.org/">All Tech Is Human</a>, an organization dedicated to collectively tackling tech and society's complex problems, aims to mitigate the risks generative AI poses to children.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">The commitment marks a significant step forward in preventing the misuse of AI technologies to create or spread child sexual abuse material (AIG-CSAM) and other forms of sexual harm against children.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">As a safety-focused organization, we have made it a priority to implement rigorous policies, conduct extensive red teaming, and collaborate with external experts to make sure our models are safe. Anthropic’s <a href="/legal/aup">policies strictly prohibit content</a> that describes, encourages, supports or distributes any form of child sexual exploitation or abuse. If we detect this material, we will report it to the National Center for Missing &amp; Exploited Children (NCMEC). It’s important to note that at this time, our models do not have multimodal outputs, even though they are able to ingest images.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">As part of this Safety by Design effort, Anthropic is <a id="base-ui-_R_v4drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://www.thorn.org/blog/generative-ai-principles/">committed to the Safety by Design principles</a>. To ensure tangible action, Anthropic is also committing to the following mitigations, stemming from the principles. We are working towards the following:</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="develop">Develop</h3><ul class="PA4F1q_reading-column text-body-1-serif post-text"><li>Responsibly source our training data: avoid ingesting data into training that has a known risk - as identified by relevant experts in the space - of containing CSAM and CSEM.</li><li>Detect, remove, and report CSAM and CSEM from our training data at ingestion.</li><li>Conduct red teaming, incorporating structured, scalable, and consistent stress testing of our models for AIG-CSAM and CSEM.</li><li>Define specific training data and model development policies.</li><li>Prohibit customer use of our models to further sexual harms against children.</li></ul><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="deploy">Deploy</h3><ul class="PA4F1q_reading-column text-body-1-serif post-text"><li>Detect abusive content (CSAM, AIG-CSAM, and CSEM) in inputs and outputs.</li><li>Include user reporting, feedback, or flagging options.</li><li>Include an enforcement mechanism.</li><li>Include prevention messaging for CSAM solicitation using available tools.</li><li>Incorporate phased deployment, monitoring for abuse in early stages before launching broadly.</li><li>Incorporate a child safety section into our model cards.</li></ul><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="maintain">Maintain</h3><ul class="PA4F1q_reading-column text-body-1-serif
