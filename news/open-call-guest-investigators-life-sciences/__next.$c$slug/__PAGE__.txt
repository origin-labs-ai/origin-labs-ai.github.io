class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Today, we’re inviting members of the life sciences community to apply to work with us on special projects. We’re looking for proposals from principal investigators and their research groups at academic institutions, nonprofits, or government labs who want to use Claude to investigate key questions in causal human biology, structural and computational molecular biology, medicinal chemistry, drug discovery, and clinical science.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">You can submit a <a href="/guest-investigator-program">proposal for a project here</a>.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Over 8–12 weeks, guest investigators will work directly with Anthropic researchers on a problem of interest and produce the analyses, artifacts, and tools that will be most helpful for them in their work. Guest investigators retain the rights to their data at every step during their project. Applicants should also make a reasonable case that their outputs (e.g., preliminary discoveries, answers to open questions, open-source datasets, or public resources) can be produced within the 8- to 12-week collaboration period. There may be an option for extension beyond the initial collaboration period for some projects.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">We understand that the results for most open problems of interest will take much longer to validate, but we’re looking for proposals that can produce some preliminary evidence in a relatively short amount of time.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">We aren’t asking for guest investigators to give us their data, either in the application or during the collaboration, and we’re requesting that applicants include only publicly available information in their application materials.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Applications will be open for approximately 14 days, and we’ll strive to notify selected participants by email approximately 14 days after applications close. We look forward to potentially expanding this effort in the future as we learn more about how we can best support the scientific community.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">This program is one of several initiatives Anthropic is pursuing to invest more heavily in the life sciences and the broader scientific research community. Scientists who would like direct access to our most capable publicly available models for biology can apply to join our <a href="/news/life-sciences-verification-program">Life Sciences Verification Program</a>.
