href="/research/team/economic-research">Economic Research</a> team is launching the Anthropic Economic Index Survey, a monthly survey conducted through <a id="base-ui-_R_1u1drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/12996960-how-does-anthropic-interviewer-collect-and-use-my-data" rel="noopener noreferrer">Anthropic Interviewer</a>.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Understanding AI's economic impact requires moving beyond the quantitative data we have today. Usage and diffusion metrics tell us how AI is being deployed, and traditional labor market indicators—like employment rates, wage trends, and layoffs—track what has already happened, often with meaningful delay. Both are essential, but neither captures how people experience the changes to the economy that AI brings, nor what they expect to happen as AI capabilities advance.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">While AI is poised to have large effects, there is substantial uncertainty about how AI will affect jobs, productivity, and unemployment (and on what timeline). To forecast a transition that is still unfolding, we need to hear from the people who are living through it, and we need to do so on a cadence that can identify changes as they emerge.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">In the Anthropic Economic Index Survey, we aim to capture a rich new corpus of qualitative data. This effort complements a <a target="_blank" href="/research/81k-economics" rel="noopener noreferrer">companion report</a> that takes an economic lens to the<a href="/features/81k-interviews"> 81,000 open-ended </a>survey responses collected through Anthropic Interviewer in December. We’ll ask Claude users about if AI is changing their work today—which tasks they may be handing off, if they’re seeing productivity gains, what shifts they may be observing in hiring and roles—their expectations for the future, and what they hope a well-handled transition looks like.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Collecting these data monthly will enable measurement of not just what people experience and expect, but how quickly their views shift as AI capabilities evolve. Combined with Claude usage data in a <a href="/research/clio">privacy-preserving way</a>, these first-hand accounts can surface change before it shows up in aggregate labor market data.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">The survey launches today. Each month, we will invite a small, randomly selected group of Claude users: anyone with a personal account at least two weeks old may be invited. We will rotate the sample each month so that we hear from as broad a range of people as possible over time. If you’re invited, you’ll see a banner on <a id="base-ui-_R_v6drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="http://claude.ai" rel="noopener noreferrer">claude.ai</a>, or get an email if you use Claude primarily on mobile. We’d love you to take part. We plan to publish insights in future Anthropic Economic Index reports and other research briefs. For more information, see the FAQ below.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently asked questions</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><strong>How do I access the study?</strong></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">From today, a small random sample of Claude users with accounts at least two weeks old will see an invitation in <a id="base-ui-_R_v9drpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="http://claude.ai" rel="noopener noreferrer">claude.ai</a>, the Cowork desktop app, or an email if you use Claude only on mobile. We invite a new set of Claude users each month, so if you’re not selected this time, keep an eye out.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><strong>What will the study ask me?</strong></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">We will use Anthropic Interviewer to ask you about your work, what changes you’ve noticed in your role or organization, how you expect that to shift over the next year, and how you hope an economy shaped by AI will look in ten years.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><strong>How will you use the data?</strong></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">We will analyze the insights from this study as part of our economic research, publish our findings, and use this to improve our models and services in ways that reflect what we’ve learned. The data we collect through this study will be processed according to our <a id="base-ui-_R_1tddrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/14139821-supplemental-anthropic-interviewer-privacy-policy" rel="noopener noreferrer">Supplemental Privacy Policy</a>. We may also include de-identified responses in published findings, from users who opt in. <a id="base-ui-_R_1uddrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/12996960-how-does-anthropic-interviewer-collect-and-use-my-data" rel="noopener noreferrer">Learn more.</a></p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><em>If you have further questions, reach out via the message icon in the lower right corner of our <a id="base-ui-_R_1uudrpbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" target="_blank" href="https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9015913-how-to-get-support" rel="noopener noreferrer">Help Center</a>.</em></p></div></div></article></div><!--$--><!--/$-->b:X
