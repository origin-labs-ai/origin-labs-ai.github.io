Audits and Certifications</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">D.1. Anthropic defines audit and risk assessment procedures for the purposes of: (a) periodic review and assessment of risks to Anthropic’s organization, assets, and data, (b) monitoring and maintaining compliance with Anthropic’s policies and procedures, and (c) reporting the condition of its information security and compliance to internal senior management.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">D.2. Anthropic’s information security program is assessed annually by independent third-party auditors and reports from such annual assessments are made available to Anthropic’s customers.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">D.3. To request access to any of our reports and certifications, please go to <a id="base-ui-_R_fjjtbsnpfkq6btb_" data-base-ui-tooltip-trigger="" href="https://trust.anthropic.com">https://trust.anthropic.com.</a></p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="e-encryption-standards">E. Encryption Standards</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Anthropic utilizes industry standard encryption methods for protection of Customer Data, including a minimum of AES-256 for data at rest, and TLS1.2+ for data in transit over public networks.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="f-endpoint-protection">F. Endpoint Protection</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">All Anthropic personnel workstations, and cloud endpoints (where applicable), are protected via EDR (endpoint detection and response) systems that include, but are not limited to: (a) anti-virus and anti-malware protections; (b) active real-time system monitoring; and (c) alerting to internal security teams.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="g-vulnerability-management">G. Vulnerability Management</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">G.1. Anthropic’s vulnerability assessment, patch management, threat protection technologies, and scheduled monitoring procedures are designed to identify, assess, mitigate, and protect against identified security threats, viruses, and other malicious code. Anthropic utilizes a multi-faceted approach to vulnerability management, including: automated code vulnerability scanning; automated artifact vulnerability scanning; automated code review; manual peer code review; endpoint detection and response; automated log analysis; and manual log analysis.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">G.2. Anthropic engages qualified external assessors for the completion of annual penetration testing of systems that process Customer Data to identify vulnerabilities and attack vectors that can be used to exploit those systems, and identified vulnerabilities are addressed as part of Anthropic’s vulnerability management program. Upon request, Anthropic will make available to Customer a summary of its most recent penetration testing report.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">G.3. Anthropic applies updates to mitigate vulnerabilities based on risk level and in alignment with industry-accepted timelines.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="h-data-management">H. Data Management</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">All Customer Data stored by Anthropic is logically separated such that no customer is able to access the data of any other customer without authorization. Customer Data associated with a specific customer is easily identifiable. Further, Anthropic maintains reasonable and appropriate network-level segmentation to separate environments processing Customer Data from the internet, from development and testing environments, and from office workspaces.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="i-logging-monitoring-and-incident-response">I. Logging, Monitoring, and Incident Response</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">I.1. Anthropic’s incident management procedures are designed to allow Anthropic to investigate, respond to, mitigate, and notify of events related to Anthropic’s technology and information assets that process Customer Data. These incident response procedures are tested annually.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">I.2. Anthropic maintains a centralized SIEM (security information and event management)/SOAR (security orchestration, automation, and response) tool.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">I.3. Anthropic logs employee access and actions within Anthropic’s systems, inclusive of those storing or maintaining Customer Data.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">I.4. System audit or event logging and related monitoring procedures are proactively configured to detect, prioritize, and escalate, as appropriate, suspicious activities for review.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">I.5. Security-related logs are retained for appropriate timeframes to aid in the investigation of security incidents.</p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="j-third-party-vendors">J. Third Party Vendors</h3><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">Anthropic maintains a third-party information security risk management program, which includes the execution of periodic risk assessments to evaluate the security posture of Anthropic’s third-party vendors.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text"><strong>Schedule 3 - International Data Transfers</strong></p><h3 class="tOQyXa_headline-5 PA4F1q_reading-column post-section" data-component="Headline" id="a-eu-sccs">A.
