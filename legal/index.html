Questions about our terms?

We’ve tried to answer the most common questions we receive below.

If you use Claude on a Free, Pro, or Max plan, the Consumer Terms of Service apply. These terms also apply to your use of Claude Code through your Claude Pro, or Max plan.

If you use the API or our Claude Team or Enterprise plans, the Commercial Terms of Service apply. These terms also apply to your use of Claude Code through the API, or Claude Team or Enterprise.

If you access Claude through Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud’s Vertex or Microsoft Foundry, you pay the marketplace provider and its terms also apply to your use.

Yes. You can grant access to your employees, affiliates’ employees, contractors, and other authorized users acting on your behalf, and you can build products that are powered by the Services for distribution to your own customers and end users. You remain responsible for your users' compliance with the Commercial Terms, and for all activity under your account.

For commercial customers (API, Claude Team or Enterprise plans, and Claude Code or Cowork accessed through them), Anthropic does not train its AI models on your inputs or outputs by default.

For Claude consumer plans (Free, Pro, Max), you choose whether your chats and coding sessions can be used to train our generative AI models. You can review or change this setting at any time in your privacy settings.

We make system cards available for all of our commercially-available models at https://www.anthropic.com/system-cards. These system cards document the capabilities, safety evaluations, and responsible deployment decisions for Claude models.

You do. As between you and Anthropic, you keep all rights to your inputs and you own the outputs the Services generate for you. Anthropic disclaims rights to your content.

Please see our Privacy Center for more details on data deletion, and our support page for export instructions.

The Data Processing Addendum is incorporated by reference into the Commercial Terms of Service and applies automatically to commercial customers.

Data processing for consumer use is governed by our Privacy Policy.

Anthropic offers a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for eligible enterprise customers using qualifying configurations of the API and Claude Enterprise. BAAs are not currently available on Free, Pro, Max, or Team plans. Read more about HIPAA and BAAs in our Help Center.

Where it is available, eligible API customers and customers using Claude Code via a Claude Enterprise plan can apply for zero data retention (ZDR). More information about ZDR functionality is available here. In the future, eligible customers will have access to Enterprise Frontier Safeguards for certain services where ZDR is not available.

If Anthropic discovers a security breach (as defined in our Data Processing Addendum) affecting a commercial customer’s data, we notify the affected customer in writing within 48 hours, as required by the DPA. We will provide you with available information about the nature and context of this security breach.

For Claude consumer plans, breach notification follows our Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Our compliance reports, like our SOC 2 Type II reports, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 certifications, audit summaries, and other compliance artifacts are available at trust.anthropic.com.

The Usage Policy sets out rules for what you can and cannot do using Claude. Examples of prohibited activity include illegal activity, weapons development, attacks on critical infrastructure, content that endangers children, fraud, election interference, and sexually explicit content. Certain high-risk uses (such as recommendations that affect an individual’s health, legal rights, finances, livelihood, or access to essential services) require additional controls, including a qualified human-in-the-loop and AI-involvement disclosure to end users.

We also require that Claude only be used in a supported region.

Yes. Anthropic monitors and engages with AI regulatory developments globally and is committed to meeting applicable obligations as they come into force. Our Transparency Hub contains a report on each of our models, with links to the full system cards and documentation on model capabilities, safety evaluations, and deployment decisions. Our Frontier Compliance Framework describes how we assess and mitigate catastrophic risks of in-scope models under applicable law, such as California’s SB 53 and the EU AI Act.

You can download the Frontier Compliance Framework, and our model documentation forms and training data summaries under the EU AI Act, from our Trust Center.

Yes. Our Code of Conduct is available on trust.anthropic.com and covers topics like ethical business conduct, anti-bribery and corruption, supplier standards, and prevention of human trafficking.

Anthropic defends and indemnifies commercial customers against third-party claims that authorized use of the Services infringes IP rights, and the IP indemnity is not subject to any liability cap. Standard carve-outs apply.

Send all legal notices to notices@anthropic.com in writing or, if you require notice by mail, by mail to Anthropic, PBC, 548 Market Street, PMB 90375, San Francisco, CA 94104.

Our published Commercial Terms apply uniformly to commercial customers and are not open to negotiation.
