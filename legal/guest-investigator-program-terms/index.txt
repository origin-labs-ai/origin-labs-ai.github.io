Program</h2><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">1.1 Program. The Program is a call for research proposals. Researchers at academic institutions, government labs and nonprofit research organizations working in biology, chemistry, drug discovery, medicine, and clinical science may submit short proposals to collaborate with Anthropic’s research team. Anthropic will select a small number of proposals at a time; for each selected proposal, Anthropic’s researchers will work with the applicant (each, a “Selected Collaborator”) on the proposed problem, and Anthropic and the Selected Collaborator intend to jointly publish the findings, analyses, artifacts, and tools that result and make them openly available to the scientific community. For each Selected Collaborator, Anthropic will provide (a) collaboration time with members of its research team and (b) work by its research team, using Anthropic’s internal models, tools, and research harnesses, on the Selected Collaborator’s problem, in each case at no cost to the Selected Collaborator. Anthropic does not provide Selected Collaborators with access to its models, compute, or other resources, and nothing in the Program grants a license to any Anthropic product or service.</p><p class="PA4F1q_reading-column post-text">1.4 What Anthropic Receives. Researchers applying on behalf of a for-profit company are not eligible. Graduate students and other student or trainee researchers may submit an application and serve as the main point of contact on behalf of a principal investigator (or equivalent) at their institution. In that case, the application must identify the principal investigator, you represent that the principal investigator has authorized the application and agreed to these Terms, and the principal investigator will be the Selected Collaborator if the application is selected.
